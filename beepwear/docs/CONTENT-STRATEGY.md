# BeepWear — Content Strategy

Master Prompt Part 8. The content plan and quality bar. Owner: the content workstream that
runs alongside M9 (policies) and feeds every page. All copy lives in `content/`.

## Principles

Original, professional, SEO-friendly, accurate, trust-building. Never Lorem Ipsum, never
placeholder paragraphs, never generic AI-style filler. Every sentence should read like an
experienced luxury watch retailer wrote it.

**Tone:** premium, professional, elegant, helpful, honest, educational, confident, friendly.
**Avoid:** clickbait, keyword stuffing, fake urgency, overpromising, aggressive sales
language, exaggerated claims. Every page helps the customer decide.

with actual operations. Field lists per Part 8 captured in each policy file's front-matter.

## FAQ (categorized → `FAQPage` schema)

## Journal strategy

Categories: Buying Guides · Luxury Lifestyle · Watch Education · Brand Stories ·
Maintenance · Industry News · Collector Guides · Style Advice · Gift Ideas. Consistent,
valuable publishing over volume. **Article template:** hero, author, date, reading time,
table of contents, body, images, pull quotes, related products, related articles,
newsletter, share buttons.

## Newsletter copy

**Headline:** Join the BeepWear Community
**Body:** Receive updates on new arrivals, buying guides, product launches, and carefully
selected offers. **Button:** Subscribe. Include the privacy notice.

## Content quality review (gate before publishing any copy)

Grammar · spelling · factual accuracy · brand tone · originality · relevant internal links ·
natural SEO · appropriate CTAs · current information · real value. Plus the Merchant Center
content check (business info visible, policies findable, no misleading claims, no fake
reviews/awards, legitimate-business presentation).
