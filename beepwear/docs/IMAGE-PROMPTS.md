# BeepWear — Editorial Image Prompts (Higgsfield AI)

Prompts for **editorial and lifestyle** imagery only — hero, banners, category art, blog
covers. **Never** for product images: product photos must accurately depict the actual
watch sold (Part 1 / Part 4). Every generated image is reviewed before publication and
must not resemble any competitor's copyrighted visuals.

## Art direction (applies to all)

Warm luxury-boutique mood; palette of deep espresso-black, warm neutrals, soft champagne-
gold light. Elegant low-key lighting, shallow depth of field, generous negative space for
text overlay, no logos, no brand names, no readable dials that imply a specific model.
Aspect ratios: hero 16:9 (and a 4:5 mobile crop), banners 21:9, category 3:4, blog 3:2.

## Hero (homepage)

> Editorial luxury still life, a single elegant wristwatch resting on brushed dark stone,
> warm champagne-gold rim light from the right, deep espresso-black background fading to
> shadow on the left for headline space, soft reflections, shallow depth of field, calm and
> premium, no text, no logo, cinematic, high detail. 16:9.

Mobile crop variant: same scene, 4:5, watch lower-right, more headroom top-left.

## Editorial / lifestyle band ("Crafted for Every Moment")

> Cinematic lifestyle scene: a person's wrist wearing a refined dress watch, resting on a
> tailored charcoal wool sleeve, warm window light, luxury interior bokeh in deep browns and
> golds, quiet and sophisticated, editorial fashion photography, no visible brand. 21:9.

## Category cards

- **Men's** — dark slate surface, masculine still life, watch beside a leather notebook,
  low warm light. 3:4.
- **Women's** — soft champagne-and-ivory palette, delicate watch on silk, airy light. 3:4.
- **Automatic** — macro of an exposed mechanical movement abstraction (no specific caliber),
  golden gears, dramatic light. 3:4.
- **Chronograph** — architectural dark background, geometric shadows, technical mood. 3:4.

## Journal covers

Match each article's subject (movements, sizing, care) with the same warm-luxury still-life
language; keep them tonal and text-safe.

## Review gate for every image

- [ ] Depicts no specific real product it isn't (no misrepresentation).
- [ ] No logos, trademarks, or competitor-lookalike composition.
- [ ] On-brand palette and lighting; leaves room for text.
- [ ] Exported as WebP/AVIF, correctly sized, descriptive alt text written.
