## Accepted payment methods

We accept: direct bank transfer (wire). Available methods are shown at checkout.

## Payment security

Payments are processed over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection by a **PCI-compliant payment
gateway** (a secure bank transfer). BeepWear does **not** store your full card details on
our servers.

## Currency

Prices are displayed and charged in **US Dollars (USD)**. [confirm: If
multi-currency is offered later, note conversion.] Your bank may apply its own conversion or
fees for international payments.

## Payment verification & fraud prevention

To protect customers, some orders may undergo verification before dispatch. We may contact
you if we need to confirm details. Orders that fail verification may be cancelled and refunded.

## Failed payments

If a payment is declined, no order is placed. Check your details and try again, or use an
alternative method. Contact your bank if the issue persists.

## Order confirmation

A successful payment produces an on-screen confirmation and an email with your order number.
If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact **info@beepwear.com**.
