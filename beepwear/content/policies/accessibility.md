## Our commitment

BeepWear is committed to making our website usable for as many people as possible, including
those who rely on assistive technologies. We aim to meet the **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** guidelines
as a practical standard.

## What we do

- Keyboard-operable navigation and visible focus indicators.
- Sufficient colour contrast and readable typography.
- Descriptive labels for forms and controls, and alternative text for meaningful images.
- Logical heading structure and semantic markup.
- Respect for reduced-motion preferences.

## Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is continuous work. We review new pages and components against these standards
and address issues as we find them.

## Reporting an issue

If you encounter a barrier or need content in an alternative format, please contact us at
