Cookies are small files stored on your device that help the site work and help us understand
how it's used. This policy explains the types we use and how to manage them.

## Types of cookies we use

- **Essential** — required for core functions: cart, checkout, security, session. The site
  can't work properly without these, so they don't require consent.
- **Analytics** — help us understand usage (pages viewed, navigation) so we can improve.
  [confirm: e.g. Google Analytics 4.] Set only with your consent.
- **Marketing** — [confirm: if used] measure campaigns and relevance. Set only with consent.
- **Preference** — remember choices like region or display settings.

## Managing cookies

On your first visit, our consent banner lets you accept or reject non-essential categories.
You can change your choice at any time via **Cookie Settings** in the footer. You can also
clear or block cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect functionality.

## Consent

Non-essential cookies are set only after you consent. We record your choice and re-ask
periodically or when this policy changes materially.
