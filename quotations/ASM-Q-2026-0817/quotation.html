Framework supply conditions

  • Volume discounts apply to the aggregate units released under a signed 12-month framework, not per shipment, so committed volume is rewarded even when drawn down in small lots.
  • Prices are held for the framework period against a fixed specification; changes in emission standard, factory list price or FX beyond ±5% are passed through and notified in advance.
  • Allocation priority and reserved production slots apply from 25 units per year, which materially shortens lead time in constrained periods.
  • Spare parts availability and consumable kits are quoted alongside each release; parts pricing is held for the framework period.
  • Brand substitution within the same tier may be offered where a factory slot is unavailable, subject to buyer's written acceptance.

Notes and qualifications

  • All prices are indicative ranges, exclusive of freight, insurance, duty, VAT and local registration or homologation.
  • This document is an offer to discuss terms, not a binding contract. A binding sale arises only on a countersigned order confirmation or proforma invoice.
