5. WHY THIS LANDS CHEAPER THAN IT LOOKS - ATIGA
------------------------------------------------------------------

Malaysia, Indonesia and Cambodia are all ASEAN member states. Refined
palm oil of Malaysian or Indonesian origin is wholly obtained within
ASEAN and therefore satisfies the ASEAN Rules of Origin.

We supply a Certificate of Origin Form D with every shipment, which
lets you claim the ATIGA preferential rate at Cambodian customs instead
of the MFN rate.

On a single 22 MT flexitank at CIF $1,230/MT - a CIF value of about
$27,060 - the difference between the 7% MFN band and the ATIGA
preferential rate is roughly $1,900 per container. Please confirm the
current rate for HS 1511.90 with the General Department of Customs and
Excise, since the applicable band should be verified against your own
tariff line. Cambodian VAT of 10% applies on CIF plus duty.

If a competing offer does not include Form D, its landed cost is not
comparable to ours.
