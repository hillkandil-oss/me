7. BEFORE YOU PLACE A 400 PC ORDER
------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT YOU NEED IN PLACE AS BUYER:

  1. State tobacco / vape retail licence
  2. Federal EIN
  3. PACT Act registration with the ATF - required to legally receive
     shipments
  4. Local business licences or municipal permits
  5. Age-verification system (21+)

WHAT APPLIES TO THE PRODUCT ITSELF:

  No high-puff flavoured disposable in this catalogue holds FDA
  marketing authorization. Roughly 14 states now run PMTA registries
  that exclude them. CBP can seize and destroy unauthorized vape
  imports at the border. Check your state's registry BEFORE choosing
  brands - it may decide the shortlist for you.

COUNTERFEIT RED FLAGS:

  Suspiciously low pricing . unverifiable business registration .
  pressure to place a large first order . inconsistent branding

  Ask every distributor for authorised-partnership confirmation and
  PMTA status documentation, and verify authenticity codes on arrival.
