Please send a formal quotation covering the following.

**1. Pricing**
- Unit price at 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 / 5,000 pcs, stated as **EXW, FOB [port], and DDP [destination]** so we
  can compare like for like.
- Currency, price validity period, and payment terms (we prefer 30% deposit / 70% against B/L copy).
- Any first-order or annual-volume incentive.

**2. Product & availability**
- Current in-stock flavours and quantities, per warehouse (China / EU / other).
- Nicotine strengths available — we require **[2% / 0% / 5%]**.
- Minimum pcs per flavour, and whether mixed-flavour cartons are possible.
- Inner box and master carton quantities, carton dimensions, gross/net weight, HS code.

**3. Lead time & logistics**
- Production/dispatch lead time from deposit, and transit time to [destination].
- Carrier options and whether customs clearance and duty are included in the DDP figure.
- Battery shipping documentation: **UN38.3 test report and MSDS** (please attach).

**4. Authenticity**
- Confirmation that you are an **authorised JNR distributor** (for resellers: please state your

- Availability of a **paid sample order of [10–20] pcs** before the volume order, and whether the sample
  cost is credited against the first bulk invoice.

**5. Compliance**
- Written confirmation of the product's regulatory status for **[destination market]**, including any
  registration, certification or authorisation the product holds there.
- Certificates you can supply: CE / RoHS / test reports / e-liquid ingredient list.

**6. Marketing assets**
- The official product image pack (studio shots, per-flavour packaging, lifestyle) in high resolution,
  with **written permission to use the images in our own listings and advertising**.

**7. After-sales**
- DOA/defect rate policy, replacement or credit terms, and who covers return freight.

We are ready to move quickly on a sample order this week. Please also confirm the best contact for
ongoing account management.
